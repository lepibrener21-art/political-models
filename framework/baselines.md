# Baselines

Reference points for comparing models. These are well-known systems, kept short on purpose.

| System | F | P | S | C | R | E | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Absolute monarchy / dictatorship | 1 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 5 | Order depends on one ruler's will; no check on abuse. |
| Constitutional monarchy | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | Monarch symbolic; power held by parliament. Denmark, Norway. |
| Representative liberal democracy | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | Elected officials, courts, rights. Voters choose people, not policies. |
| Direct democracy (Swiss-style, mixed) | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | Referendums alongside parliament. Women's federal vote only in 1971. |
| Pure direct democracy (Athenian) | 2 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Small citizen body; majorities could exile or execute. |
| Statelessness without institutions | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | Power vacuum tends to be filled by the strongest actor. |

Key lesson from the baselines: **the label matters less than whether power is dispersed and constrained.**
