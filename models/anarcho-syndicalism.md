# Anarchism (syndicalist and communal forms)

**In one line:** no coercive state; society organized through voluntary federations of workers' unions, communes and cooperatives.

## Mechanism
- Workplaces run by workers; communities by assemblies.
- Federations coordinate upward by mandated delegates.
- No standing army or police in principle; defense by popular militia.
- Other branches: mutualism (Proudhon), anarcho-capitalism (Rothbard), which replaces the state with markets and private agencies.

## Origins
- Proudhon, Bakunin, Kropotkin [kropotkin1902].

## Real-world use
- Revolutionary Catalonia, 1936–39 (CNT): collectivized industry and farms; crushed in the Spanish Civil War.
- Free Territory, Ukraine, 1918–21 (Makhno): defeated by the Red Army.
- Related evidence: worker cooperatives such as Mondragón (Spain) show worker self-management can work inside a state.
- Anthropology of stateless societies [graeber2021, scott2009].

## Strengths
- Maximum personal freedom and popular power in theory.
- Removes the main tool of mass atrocity: a centralized state.

## Weaknesses
- Power vacuum and defense: almost every experiment was destroyed by organized states.
- Informal hierarchies replace formal ones, without accountability [freeman1972].
- Coordination at scale among millions of strangers is unsolved.

## Open questions
- Can digital coordination tools solve the scale problem?
