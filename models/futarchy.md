# Futarchy

**In one line:** "vote on values, bet on beliefs": citizens vote on goals, and prediction markets decide which policies best achieve them.

## Mechanism
- Voters choose a measurable welfare target (e.g. a mix of GDP, health, happiness).
- For each proposed policy, markets trade on what the target will be with and without it.
- The policy the markets predict will score better is adopted.

## Origins
- Robin Hanson, 2000 [hanson2000].

## Real-world use
- No government use. Small experiments in blockchain organizations (e.g. MetaDAO, from 2023).
- Prediction markets have a mixed but often good forecasting record.

## Strengths
- Rewards accurate prediction and punishes wishful thinking.
- Separates disagreement about goals from disagreement about facts.

## Weaknesses
- Rich participants can manipulate markets to push policies.
- Hard to turn values into one measurable number without losing what matters.
- Popular power shrinks to choosing the metric.

## Open questions
- Could it work as an advisory layer only, informing legislators?
